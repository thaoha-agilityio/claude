# Expense Tracker – MVP

## Features

- Create, edit, and delete expenses (amount, date, category, and notes).
- Manage categories.
- Monthly reports: total expenses and expenses by category.

## Constraints

- **Currency:** VND, no decimal places.
- **Time zone:** Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.
- **Tech stack:** React.

## Out of Scope for the MVP

- Budgeting.
- Excel export.
- Sharing.
